Rave stabs for the SAMPLE engine (STAB sets, tools/gen_samples.py).
Sixteen one-shot stabs supplied by the project owner (Ben Mathers) from his own sound design.
Mono, 16 bit, 44.1 kHz (the bounces were 32-bit float stereo), trimmed to the sound, normalised and faded out.
File name: NN_NAME.wav; NAME (at most 5 characters) is the SET name on the device; NN the order.
All are played at their natural pitch on MIDI note 60 (middle C) and pitched across the keyboard.
